# Governance

## Experimental phase

OGIR begins as a maintainer-led research project. Architectural decisions are recorded publicly in `docs/adr/`, except for temporary embargoed vulnerability details.

## Decision principles

1. Security and privacy claims must be explicit and testable.
2. No contributor, sponsor, publisher, or maintainer may privately weaken an invariant.
3. Commercial sponsorship does not grant merge authority.
4. A publisher controls its verifier policy and match authorization, but not the local user's unrelated system data.
5. Protocol and conformance requirements remain publicly reviewable.

## Path to shared governance

Before production adoption by multiple organizations, OGIR should establish:

- a technical steering committee;
- a security response committee;
- conflict-of-interest rules;
- multi-party control of release and policy-signing roots;
- a public trademark and certification policy;
- a neutral process for protocol and conformance changes;
- a funded, independently administered security reserve.
