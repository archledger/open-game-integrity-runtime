## Development notes (every one empirically forced)

- The wine-11 loader map (six points, in ADR-0029): WINEDLLPATH
  dead for imports; LoadLibraryA never resolves winelib artifacts;
  override=n kills the unixlib attach (the attach runs in the
  unforced n,b phase); a dispatch-only .so in the machine-unix dir
  loads AS the module and stub-aborts without real spec exports; an
  UNINITIALIZED dispatch table attaches nothing (the gate now
  asserts data, not bss).
- wine refuses a WINEPREFIX under /tmp ("not owned by you") - use a
  home-path prefix.
- The header's default-convention declarations collide with ms_abi
  definitions: export through ms_abi wrappers with distinct names,
  bound by the spec's third token.
- The spike's run-spike.sh carried a corrupted 'cd .../ rundir'
  (space) for most of its life - the greens ran from the spike
  root, which is why the native+system-unixlib attach looked
  mysterious until the strace and the corrupted cd were found
  together. Lesson: verify the deployment context a script claims
  (pwd!) before drawing loader conclusions from its output.
- A stale system-dir .so shadows fresh local builds silently -
  always re-verify the installed artifact's symbols after a copy
  (nm -D) before rerunning.
- The 32-bit PE needs its own i686 build (0x014C); the wow64
  dispatch of custom arg structs needs a conversion layer - the
  prototype fails closed there by design.
- portal-serve exits after 8 rounds; long debug sessions exhausted
  it silently (ECONNREFUSED mid-loop).
